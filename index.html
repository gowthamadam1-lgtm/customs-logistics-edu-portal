        Getting started

        How do I get access?

        Create an account from the login screen. If we ask you to confirm your email, click the link we send you. A staff member then approves your account; until they do, logging in says your account is awaiting approval.

        I forgot my password

        Ask a staff member to set a new one for you. Once you're logged in, you can change it any time on the Account page.

        What does “Remember me” do?

        It keeps you logged in on this device after you close the browser. Untick it on a shared or public computer.

        Courses and topics

        Where do I start?

        Home shows your next checkpoint: the first topic you haven't completed yet. Tap its button to carry on where you left off. Your courses are listed below it, each with how many topics you've completed.

        How does a topic work?

        Open the topic and go through its learning material: documents, videos, photos or reading text. When you've finished, tap Mark Learning Material as Completed. That unlocks the topic's quiz.

        What's in Other Courses?

        Extra courses on top of the ones on Home. They work the same way.

        KPI reports

        How do I upload my KPI report?

        Open Update KPI Report, give it a title (and notes if you like), choose your PowerPoint file (.ppt or .pptx, up to 50 MB) and tap Upload KPI report. Staff review every report.

        Can I remove a report?

        Yes. Your reports are listed on the same page; choose Delete on the one you want to remove.

          Exams

          Every topic quiz in one place. Finished tests show Passed in green or Failed in red; the pass mark is 60%. You can revise any test at any time. A revision is a practice attempt, so your recorded score stays as it is.
