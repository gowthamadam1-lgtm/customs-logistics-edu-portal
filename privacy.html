Who we are

CLE Portal is run by [Your name or organisation]. If you have any question about this policy or your information, email [contact email].

What we collect

  • Your account: the username and email address you sign up with, and your password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form; we cannot read it).
  • Your profile photo, if you upload one.
  • Your learning record: which topics and learning materials you have completed, your quiz scores, your points and your rank. Your quiz answers are sent to our server to be marked; we keep the score, not your individual answers.
  • KPI reports (PowerPoint files) you upload, with the title and notes you give them.
  • Support tickets and discussions: the questions you raise and the replies you write.
  • Technical data that our hosting provider records when you sign in, such as the time of your last sign-in, to keep the service secure.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos other than the ones you choose to upload, or payment details. There are no adverts and no advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in, take courses and quizzes, and track your progress.
  • To show the leaderboard, raise and answer support tickets, and let staff review KPI reports.
  • To keep the portal secure, for example by approving new accounts before they can sign in.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Other learners can see your username, profile photo, points and rank on the leaderboard, and any discussion you post that you do not mark as private.
  • Staff (portal administrators) can see your account details, progress, KPI reports and all your tickets, including private ones, so they can run the courses and help you.
  • Help contacts: the name, role, email address and mobile number that staff add to the Help page are visible to every signed-in learner.
  • Service providers that run the portal for us:
    • Supabase stores the database, sign-in and uploaded files (hosted in Sydney, Australia).
    • When you open a document preview, Microsoft's Office online viewer receives a temporary link to that file so it can display it.
    • Google Fonts and the unpkg network deliver the portal's fonts and code; like any website, they see your device's internet address when they do.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When your account is deleted, your information is deleted with it, as described below.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and data yourself at any time:

  1. Sign in to CLE Portal (website or app).
  2. Open Account from the menu.
  3. Choose Delete my account, type your username to confirm, and choose Delete permanently.

This permanently deletes your login, profile, profile photo, learning record, points, KPI reports and your own support tickets straight away. Replies you wrote on other people's tickets stay, without your name. If you cannot sign in, email [contact email] from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing [contact email]. You can change your username, password and profile photo yourself on the Account page.

Children

CLE Portal is meant for adult learners and professionals and is not directed at children under 13.

Security

All traffic to the portal is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to your information is restricted by account rules in the database, and quiz marking and account changes happen on our server, not in your browser.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
